Bedside and desk clock apps: subscription or one-time unlock?

An old iPhone or iPad on a charger makes a good desk or bedside clock. Most clock apps are free to download, but they charge in very different ways. Some sell one cheap unlock, some sell a dozen small add-ons, and a few sell subscriptions. Here is what we found, including the parts that don’t flatter us.

To be fair: three of these four popular apps already sell one-time purchases, and their add-ons can cost less than our unlock. Avoiding a subscription is not hard in this category. The real differences are ads, how many separate purchases you need, and how the clock behaves at night.

What recent low-star reviews mention most

We read the most recent public reviews rated 1–3 stars for each app on the US store. Common themes:

  • Ads. This was the most common complaint for the bedside alarm app, including ads at the bottom of the screen and pop-ups while using the clock.
  • Paying to set it up. Reviewers describe customizing a clock and only then finding that the settings they wanted are locked. One review put it as “You have to pay, just to look at the time?”
  • Not knowing what you are buying. When an app has several purchases, some reviewers are unsure which one unlocks what, or say a purchase didn’t unlock what they expected.
  • Behavior at night. Some reviewers say the screen switches off, or say the clock is too bright for the bedroom.

Where Stay Clock is a good fit

  • One optional purchase. The Lifetime unlock opens every face. There are no separate add-ons and no renewal.
  • Made for the bedroom: a dim red face on true black for night use, plus a Keep Awake setting that knows when the phone is charging (stay on while charging, stay on down to a battery level you choose, or follow the system).

  • iPhone and iPad, any orientation: full-screen time in portrait or landscape, with 12- or 24-hour time, any time zone and an hourly chime.

Where Stay Clock is not the right choice

  • The free version is very limited. You get one face. If you want more than that, you’ll need the Lifetime unlock. If one face is enough, you never pay.
  • It may cost more than a single add-on elsewhere. If you only want one extra style, the small one-time purchases in the apps above may be cheaper.
  • A simple alarm, not an alarm-clock app. Stay Clock has a basic alarm. If you need several alarms, snoozes or sleep tracking, a dedicated alarm app will do more.
  • A screen that stays on still uses power. Keep the device on a charger. Pixel shifting lowers the risk of OLED burn-in but can’t remove it completely.
  • It’s new. Stay Clock has no App Store ratings yet.

A quick way to compare any clock app

  1. Open the app’s App Store page and scroll to In-App Purchases. “Monthly” or “Yearly” means a subscription. A long list of small items means several purchases.
  2. Look for a “Remove Ads” purchase. If there is one, the free version shows ads.
  3. Test it at night. Put the phone where you sleep and check the brightness and whether the screen stays on before you pay.
