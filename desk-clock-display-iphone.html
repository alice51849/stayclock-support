Set up a desk clock display in four steps

  1. Pick the device. An iPhone you still use, an old iPhone in a drawer, or a spare iPad all work; there are layouts for every iPhone and iPad screen size.
  2. Give it a spot and power. A charging stand on the desk, a shelf or the nightstand. Portrait and landscape both work.
  3. Choose how it stays on. In Keep Awake, pick stay on while charging, always on with a battery floor, or follow the system.
  4. Choose a face and a calendar. Pick a face, then Off, Simple (date and weekday) or Month for that face. Set 12 or 24 hour time, any city or time zone, and your own digits.

Frequently asked questions

How do I turn an old iPhone into a desk clock display?

Put it on a charger or stand where you can see it, install Stay Clock, open the app and pick a face. Set Keep Awake to stay on while charging, so the full-screen time stays lit while the phone charges on the desk.

Can the clock display stay on without being plugged in?

Yes. Keep Awake can stay on while charging, stay always on with a battery floor, or follow the system setting.

Will it work in portrait, landscape and on iPad?

Yes. The full-screen time stays readable from far away in portrait or landscape, with iPhone and iPad layouts for every screen size.

Can a desk clock display show the date too?

Yes. Each face can show no calendar, a Simple date with the weekday, or a full Month view, saved separately for every face.

Is it dark enough for a bedroom?

There is a night schedule that switches to a dim red face on true black, and Panel Care adds gentle pixel shifting for OLED screens.

Does it cost anything?

Stay Clock is a free download with one complete face. One optional Lifetime purchase unlocks 100 more faces for good, with no subscription.
