Frequently asked questions

Does Stay Clock work in StandBy?

Yes. The face you pick appears in StandBy as well as on the Lock Screen, Home Screen widgets and Apple Watch, and looks the same on each.

Can I use it as a bedside clock on an OLED iPhone?

Yes. Set a night schedule and Stay Clock switches to a dim red face on true black made for the bedroom, while Panel Care gently shifts pixels and softens idle details to look after OLED screens. Keep Awake can hold the screen on while charging, so the bedside clock stays readable through the night.

Why use the app instead of only StandBy?

The full-screen app works in portrait or landscape on iPhone and iPad, shows a calendar that suits each face (Off, Simple with date and weekday, or a full Month view), and lets you decide when the screen stays awake.

Will the screen stay on all night?

Keep Awake understands charging: stay on while charging, stay always on above a battery floor you set, or follow the system setting, so the clock never surprises you.

Is it safe for an OLED screen at night?

Panel Care uses gentle pixel shifting and details that soften when idle, and the night schedule switches to a dim red face on true black for the bedroom.

Can it chime or wake me up?

There is an hourly chime and a simple alarm. You can also choose the app language, any city or time zone, 12 or 24 hour time and your own digits.

Does it need an account or the internet?

No. No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no network. Everything stays on your device.
