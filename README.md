# Stay Clock — support and privacy

Generated pages for the App Store listing of Stay Clock (iOS, iPadOS, watchOS).

- `index.html` — what the app does, what Lifetime unlocks, privacy summary, contact
- `support.html` — getting started, frequently asked questions, contact
- `privacy.html` — privacy policy

All three pages carry all 50 App Store locales inline and switch on the visitor's
language automatically, with a language menu at the top right. No framework, no CDN,
no font request, no analytics, no cookie, no third-party request of any kind.

Never hand-edit these files. They are rendered from the single source of truth in the
app repository (`marketing/src/copy/*.json`) by `marketing/build.py`, then copied here.
