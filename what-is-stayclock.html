What is Stay Clock?

Stay Clock is an iPhone and iPad app that turns the device into an always-on desk clock you can read from across the room, in portrait or landscape. It offers 101 faces, flip clocks included, a dim red face on true black for night, and the same face on StandBy, Lock Screen and Home Screen widgets and Apple Watch. Each face can show no calendar, a simple date and weekday, or a month view. It also has an hourly chime, a simple alarm, any city or time zone, and 12 or 24 hour time.

Who is Stay Clock for?

People who put an old iPhone or iPad on a desk, shelf or nightstand as a clock, people who use StandBy while charging, and anyone who wants a bedside clock that is not glaring at night. Keep Awake can stay on while charging, stay always on with a battery floor, or follow the system, and Panel Care uses gentle pixel shifting to look after OLED screens.

How does Stay Clock charge?

Stay Clock is a free download with one complete face, a handwritten paper look. One optional Lifetime purchase unlocks 100 more faces for good, with no subscription. There are no ads. For the current price, check the App Store page, because prices differ by country.

How is Stay Clock different from other clock apps?

Based on what the listing describes: the face you pick looks the same on the full-screen clock, StandBy, Lock Screen and Home Screen widgets and Apple Watch; the calendar style is saved separately for each face; Keep Awake understands charging; there is OLED care with a night schedule; and it has no account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no network.
