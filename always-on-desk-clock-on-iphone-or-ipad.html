Turn an iPhone or iPad into an always-on desk or bedside clock

A phone on a charging stand or a spare iPad on the shelf makes a good clock, as long as three things are right: it stays on when you want it to, you can read it from across the room, and it does not light up the bedroom or wear out the screen at night. Here is how to set that up, when the built-in StandBy view is all you need, and when a dedicated clock app is the better fit.

Is StandBy enough?

Since iOS 17, iPhone has StandBy: a full-screen view that appears while the phone is charging, locked and turned on its side. If your iPhone already sits in landscape on a charger at night, StandBy may be all you need.

A clock app earns its place when you want something StandBy does not do for you: a clock in portrait as well as landscape, a full-screen clock on an iPad, a clock that keeps running on battery with a floor you choose, a calendar laid out the way you like, or the same clock design on the Lock Screen, Home Screen widgets and your Apple Watch.

Night use without burning a hole in the screen or your sleep

An always-on clock is a bright rectangle in a dark room, and on OLED screens a static image shown for hours is the thing to avoid. Two habits help: use a dim face on true black at night, so the panel shows as few lit pixels as possible, and let the layout move slightly over time instead of holding every pixel in place. Stay Clock’s Panel Care does both with pixel shifting, idle softening and the dim red night face, and the night schedule switches to it on its own.
