Set up a desk clock display in four steps

  1. Pick the device. The old iPhone you upgraded from, a spare iPad or the phone you use every day all work; there are layouts for every iPhone and iPad screen size.
  2. Give it a spot and power. A charging stand on the desk, the bedside table or a shelf. Portrait and landscape both work.
  3. Pick your city or time zone. Choose any city or time zone inside the app, plus 12 or 24 hour time and your own digits.
  4. Choose how it stays on and a face. In Keep Awake, pick stay on while charging, always on with a battery floor, or follow the system. Then pick a face and Off, Simple (date and weekday) or Month for that face.

Frequently asked questions

Can the clock show the time in another city or time zone?

Yes. You can pick any city or time zone inside the app, so a spare iPad on the desk can keep the time somewhere else, such as where family live interstate or overseas.

How do I turn my old iPhone into a desk clock display?

Put it on a charger or stand where you can see it, install Stay Clock, open the app and pick a face. Set Keep Awake to stay on while charging, so the full-screen time stays lit while the phone charges on the desk.

Can it stay on without being plugged in?

Yes. Keep Awake can stay on while charging, stay always on with a battery floor, or follow the system setting.

Is it dark enough for the bedside table?

There is a night schedule that switches to a dim red face on true black, and Panel Care adds gentle pixel shifting for OLED screens.

Is there a flip clock look?

Yes. Flip clocks are part of the 101 faces. The free face has a handwritten paper look, and the flip clock faces come with the optional Lifetime unlock, which adds 100 more faces in different colours, styles and fonts.

Can it show the date as well?

Yes. Each face can show no calendar, a Simple date with the weekday, or a full Month view, saved separately for every face.

Will it work in landscape and on iPad?

Yes. The full-screen time stays readable from far away in portrait or landscape, with iPhone and iPad layouts for every screen size.

Does it cost anything?

Stay Clock is a free download with one complete face. One optional Lifetime purchase unlocks 100 more faces for good, with no subscription.

Will ads ever cover the clock?

No. Stay Clock has no ads, no account, no analytics and no tracking, and it needs no network. Everything stays on your device.
