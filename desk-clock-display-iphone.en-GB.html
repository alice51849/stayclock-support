Desk clock display: turn an iPhone or iPad into a bedside or desk clock

That old iPhone in the kitchen drawer or the iPad you no longer use already has a bright screen and a charger. With a clock app it becomes a desk clock display for the bedside table or the home-office desk: big full-screen time you can read from across the room, shown in 24-hour format if you like, that stays on while it charges and goes dim red at night.

Set up a bedside or desk clock display in four steps

  1. Pick the device. An old iPhone from a drawer, a spare iPad or the phone you use every day all work; there are layouts for every iPhone and iPad screen size.
  2. Give it a spot and power. A charging stand on the bedside table, the home-office desk or a shelf. Portrait and landscape both work.
  3. Set 24-hour time and your digits. Choose 12 or 24 hour time and your own digits, and switch the app language instantly inside the app.
  4. Choose how it stays on and a face. In Keep Awake, pick stay on while charging, always on with a battery floor, or follow the system. Then pick a face and Off, Simple (date and weekday) or Month for that face.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use an old iPhone as a bedside clock?

Yes. Put it on a charger on the bedside table, install Stay Clock and pick a face. Set Keep Awake to stay on while charging, and a night schedule switches it to a dim red face on true black so it does not light up the bedroom.

Can it show the 24-hour clock?

Yes. You can choose 12 or 24 hour time and your own digits, and pick any city or time zone inside the app.

Is there a flip clock look?

Yes. Flip clocks are part of the 101 faces. The free face has a handwritten paper look, and the flip clock faces come with the optional Lifetime unlock, which adds 100 more faces in different colours, styles and fonts.

Will it stay on if it is not plugged in?

Yes. Keep Awake can stay on while charging, stay always on with a battery floor, or follow the system setting.

Can a desk clock display show the date too?

Yes. Each face can show no calendar, a Simple date with the weekday, or a full Month view, saved separately for every face.

Does it work on an iPad in landscape on the desk?

Yes. The full-screen time stays readable from far away in portrait or landscape, with iPhone and iPad layouts for every screen size.

Will my Lock Screen and Home Screen widgets match?

Yes. The face you pick appears on the full-screen clock, in StandBy, in Lock Screen and Home Screen widgets and on Apple Watch, and looks the same on every one.

Does it cost anything?

Stay Clock is a free download with one complete face. One optional Lifetime purchase unlocks 100 more faces for good, with no subscription.

Are there ads or an account?

No. Stay Clock has no ads, no account, no analytics and no tracking, and it needs no network. Everything stays on your device.
